## 📊 Insights & Conclusions

### Key Insights

1. **Overall View → Cart conversion is 6.19%**, indicating that only a small proportion of product viewers add an item to their cart.

2. **Cart → Purchase conversion is 49.98%**, showing that nearly half of the users who add products to their cart eventually make a purchase.

3. **Overall View → Purchase conversion is 6.82%**, highlighting significant potential for improving the early stages of the customer journey.

4. **View → Cart is the major funnel bottleneck.** Since only 6.19% of viewers reach the cart, improving product-page engagement could have the biggest impact on overall conversions.

5. At the top-category level, **Medicine has the highest View → Cart conversion (15.79%)**, followed by Electronics (9.79%).

6. **Sport has the highest Cart → Purchase conversion (75%)**, followed by Medicine (66.67%) and Kids (55.56%).

7. **Medicine achieves the highest View → Purchase conversion (10.53%)** among the major categories analyzed.

8. At the sub-category level, **Tonometer has the highest View → Cart conversion (15.79%)**, followed by Camera (12.50%) and Smartphone (10.69%).

9. **Tonometer also has the highest View → Purchase conversion (10.53%)**, making it one of the strongest-performing sub-categories.

10. Several sub-categories — including **Bicycle, Light, Steam Cooker, Coffee Machine and Motherboard — show 100% Cart → Purchase conversion** in the analyzed data.

11. At the brand level, **Diamond has the highest View → Cart conversion (33.33%)**, followed by Adata (25%) and EA (22.73%).

12. **Diamond also has the highest View → Purchase conversion (33.33%)**, making it the strongest-performing brand according to this metric.

13. The highest **View → Cart drop-offs** among top categories are concentrated in areas with relatively weak conversion performance, indicating opportunities for better product presentation and customer engagement.

14. **Furniture has the highest View → Purchase drop-off (99.86%)** among the top categories, suggesting a major opportunity for improvement.

15. At the sub-category level, **Table (99.45%), Bed (99.38%) and Skates (99.29%)** have some of the highest View → Cart drop-off rates.

16. At the brand level, **Triangle (99.74%) and Pioneer (99.67%)** have particularly high View → Cart drop-off rates.

17. Some products show **100% conversion at individual funnel stages**. These results should be interpreted carefully because high percentages can occur when the number of sessions for a product is small.

18. The analysis demonstrates that funnel performance varies considerably across **categories, sub-categories, brands and individual products**, making segmentation important for identifying conversion opportunities.

### 💡 Business Recommendations

* Improve **product-page design, descriptions, images and pricing** to increase View → Cart conversion.
* Use **personalized recommendations and related products** to encourage users to add items to their carts.
* Investigate categories and products with **very high drop-off rates** to identify pricing, product-quality or UX issues.
* Analyze high-performing categories such as **Medicine and Electronics** to identify practices that can be applied to weaker categories.
* Optimize the **cart and checkout experience** for segments with high Cart → Purchase drop-off.
* Use high-performing brands and products as benchmarks for improving underperforming segments.
* Consider applying a **minimum session threshold** before ranking products or brands, since 100% conversion can be misleading for low-volume items.

## 🎯 Final Conclusion

The funnel analysis shows that the **largest opportunity lies in converting product views into cart additions**. While the View → Cart conversion is only **6.19%**, the Cart → Purchase conversion reaches **49.98%**, indicating that users who add products to their carts already demonstrate strong purchase intent.

Therefore, the primary business focus should be on **improving product discovery, product-page engagement and the View → Cart stage**. Category, sub-category, brand and product-level analysis can then be used to identify high-performing segments and target underperforming segments with specific improvements.

Overall, this analysis provides a **data-driven view of customer behavior and conversion performance**, helping businesses identify funnel bottlenecks and opportunities to increase purchases.
